Why it's different

Most "file organizers" only look at the file name.
ClerkBrain actually reads the document.

That's the difference between a tool that sorts .pdf files into a PDF folder, and one that knows a file is an electricity bill from March, due in two weeks.

🧠

Reads the content, not just the name

Local text extraction and OCR turn PDFs, scans and photos into text, so a blurry phone photo of a receipt is understood the same as a clean PDF invoice.

✅

Double-checks itself

An on-device AI re-reads flagged documents and compares its answer to the rules-based extraction, so you know when a total or date needs a second look.

🔒

Never leaves your PC

No account, no cloud upload, no telemetry. The only network call is an optional, one-time, SHA-256-verified download of the AI models themselves.

What you can do

Four things people actually search for — solved

NEEDS ATTENTION

Only the documents that actually need you

Most files are read, classified and filed quietly in the background. The ones left for you are the ones with a real reason: a total that doesn't add up, a blurry scan, or a missing invoice number — each explained in plain English, not an error code.

  • AI double-check compares its own reading against the rules-based one
  • "Fill in" a corrected value with one click — always reversible
  • Nothing is ever silently wrong: unclear values are flagged, not guessed
ASK

Ask a question, get a sourced answer

"How much was my last electricity bill?" "Which invoices did I get from Acme?" Type it in plain language and the on-device AI searches your documents by meaning (not just matching words) and answers with the exact source file shown underneath.

  • Answers always cite the document they came from — nothing is invented
  • Finds files by what they mean, not only the words they contain
  • Runs locally once the search/answer models are downloaded (your choice, optional)
ORGANIZE

Clear names and tidy folders — only when you say so

A folder full of scan0042.pdf and IMG_2231.png becomes 2026-03-12_Acme Plumbing_Invoice_INV-2041_£312.00.pdf, filed under Organized\Finance\2026\Invoice\. Nothing moves until you tick it and apply — and every change shows up in Undo History, reversible any time.

  • Suggestions only — review before anything renames or moves
  • Every applied change is logged and can be undone
  • Duplicate files are cleaned up first, so suggestions don't collide
CLEANUP

Find duplicate files and old installers safely

A quick scan finds exact duplicates and stale leftovers cluttering your folders. You choose what goes — everything is sent to the Recycle Bin, never deleted outright, so a mis-click is never permanent.

  • Preview any candidate before deciding
  • Recycle Bin, not permanent delete — always restorable
  • Pairs with Organize once duplicates are cleared
